# Abstract
With the increasing evolution of e-commerce in Bangladesh, online shopping has become
a necessary commodity of daily life. With more customers turning to online shopping more
than ever, so has the amount of reviews for various products and services. But reading
hundreds of reviews to pull insights to make better decisions is a full-time job for Companies.
Besides, it becomes a laborious process for the e-commerce business to analyze these reviews
to enhance its service and products. In this work, we endeavor to perform sentiment analysis
and classification of customer reviews based on Bangladeshi e-commerce platforms through
convolutional neural networks CNN and recurrent convolutional neural networks RCNN.
The dataset only in bangla Language consists of 2,866 reviews: 880 products, 1986 delivery
associated value, and 1479 positive, 1387 negative values. Moreover, we opted to use CNN
and RCNN because they performed well on smaller datasets. The models were able to give
very good performance on Classification (Product or Delivery) related reviews and Sentiment
(Positive or Negative) Analysis on reviews with CNN giving 87% accuracy for classification
and 91% accuracy for sentiment analysis. RCNN gave a little bit better even result of 90%
in classification and 91% in sentiment analysis. Even with such limited data these results
demonstrate that deep learning models can produce reasonable results. This experiment is
useful for the analysis of Bangla text in both references to evaluating E-commerce data using
deep learning techniques. It also serves as a foundation for further research on the customer
reviews in resource-limited environments
